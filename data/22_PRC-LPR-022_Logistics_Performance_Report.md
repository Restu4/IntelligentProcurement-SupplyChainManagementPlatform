## Purpose

This report evaluates the performance of PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia's inbound and outbound logistics service providers against contracted SLA metrics for Q1 2026, supporting freight cost management and 3PL contract governance.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Logistics Network Overview
PT NMI's logistics network comprises inbound raw material transport (primarily road and limited coastal shipping), outbound finished goods distribution to 11 regional distribution centers, and cold chain logistics for temperature-sensitive ingredients. Key logistics partners include PT Sentosa Cold Chain Logistics (cold chain), CV Berkat Logistik Indonesia (domestic trucking, Java network), and PT Tunas Logistik Mandiri (outer-island distribution, including Sumatra and Sulawesi).

### 2. Q1 2026 Performance Summary

| Provider | On-Time Delivery % | Damage/Loss Rate | Freight Cost vs. Budget | Fleet Utilization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CV Berkat Logistik Indonesia | 96.4% | 0.18% | -2.1% (favorable) | 87% |
| PT Sentosa Cold Chain Logistics | 93.8% | 0.31% | +1.4% | 81% |
| PT Tunas Logistik Mandiri | 89.2% | 0.52% | +6.8% | 74% |

### 3. CV Berkat Logistik Indonesia (Java Domestic Trucking)
Delivered strong performance with 96.4% on-time delivery against the contracted 95% SLA target, and a damage/loss rate of 0.18%, well within the 0.5% contractual ceiling. Freight costs came in 2.1% favorable to budget due to improved route optimization and backhaul utilization (carrying outbound finished goods and inbound packaging materials on the same circuit where feasible). This provider remains the Company's primary domestic trucking partner with no escalations required.

### 4. PT Sentosa Cold Chain Logistics
On-time delivery of 93.8% fell marginally short of the 95% SLA target, attributed to two incidents of refrigeration unit malfunction during transit (both resolved with product integrity maintained per temperature logging data, no product loss). Freight cost variance of +1.4% reflects fuel surcharge pass-through consistent with contractual index-linked pricing. Damage/loss rate of 0.31% remains within the 0.4% contractual ceiling for cold chain operations. A minor service review has been scheduled to address the refrigeration reliability concern.

### 5. PT Tunas Logistik Mandiri (Outer-Island Distribution)
This provider underperformed against both delivery and cost targets. On-time delivery of 89.2% fell materially below the 95% SLA target, driven by recurring vessel scheduling delays affecting the Java-Sumatra and Java-Sulawesi shipping routes during the period. Freight cost overrun of 6.8% versus budget reflects unplanned air freight substitution used to meet critical distribution center replenishment deadlines when sea freight was delayed. Damage/loss rate of 0.52% exceeds the 0.4% contractual ceiling, attributed to handling issues during port transshipment. A formal Service Improvement Notice has been issued under the SLA Agreement (PRC-SLA-023 framework), requiring a corrective action plan within 30 days.

### 6. Freight Cost Analysis
Total logistics spend for Q1 2026 was Rp 98,400,000,000 against a budget of Rp 94,800,000,000 (3.8% unfavorable variance), driven primarily by the PT Tunas Logistik Mandiri air freight substitution costs (Rp 6,200,000,000 incremental) partially offset by favorable performance from CV Berkat Logistik Indonesia.

### 7. Fleet Utilization and Capacity Planning
Fleet utilization rates (percentage of available truck/vessel capacity actually used) ranged from 74% (PT Tunas Logistik Mandiri) to 87% (CV Berkat Logistik Indonesia). The Logistics Department is evaluating consolidated shipment scheduling to improve utilization for outer-island routes, potentially reducing per-unit freight cost by an estimated 8–12% if implemented successfully in H2 2026.

## Rules

1. Any 3PL provider falling below 90% on-time delivery for a full quarter automatically triggers a Service Improvement Notice under the governing SLA Agreement.
2. Damage/loss rates exceeding the contractual ceiling for any single month require immediate root-cause investigation, not only quarterly reporting.
3. Freight cost variance exceeding 5% unfavorable for any provider requires a written cost driver explanation submitted to the Logistics Department within 10 working days of quarter-end.
4. Unplanned modal substitution (e.g., air freight replacing sea freight) above Rp 1,000,000,000 cumulative in a quarter requires Logistics Department Head notification to the CFO given the budget impact.

## Procedures

1. Logistics Performance Manager extracts delivery, damage, and cost data from the Transportation Management System (TMS) and SAP.
2. Performance calculated against contracted SLA metrics per provider.
3. Underperformance triggers Service Improvement Notice workflow per the relevant SLA Agreement.
4. Quarterly Logistics Performance Report reviewed by Supply Chain Director and shared with Procurement Steering Committee.
5. Corrective action plans tracked to closure by the Logistics Department.

## Thresholds

| Metric | SLA Target | Breach Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| On-Time Delivery | ≥95% | <90% for full quarter → Service Improvement Notice |
| Damage/Loss Rate (ambient) | ≤0.5% | Exceeded in any month → Investigation |
| Damage/Loss Rate (cold chain) | ≤0.4% | Exceeded in any month → Investigation |
| Freight Cost Variance | ±3% | >5% unfavorable → Written explanation required |

## Penalties

- Sustained SLA breach beyond the cure period under the Service Improvement Notice results in Service Level Credits per the SLA Agreement (PRC-SLA-023), typically 0.5–1.5% of monthly freight invoice value per metric breached.
- Failure to remediate within the agreed corrective action timeline authorizes the Logistics Department to initiate competitive re-tender of the affected lane/route without further negotiation extension.
- Unauthorized modal substitution without proper notification (per Rule 4) is treated as a budget control gap, reviewed jointly by Logistics and Finance.

## Approval Levels

Service Improvement Notice issuance: Logistics Performance Manager initiates, Logistics Department Head approves. Re-tender of a logistics lane due to non-remediation: Supply Chain Director approval. Modal substitution cost above Rp 1,000,000,000: CFO notification required, Logistics Department Head approval to proceed.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: If PT Tunas Logistik Mandiri fails to improve on-time delivery above 93% within the 30-day corrective action window, the Logistics Department will initiate a competitive re-tender for the outer-island distribution lanes, potentially engaging an alternate provider while maintaining PT Tunas Logistik Mandiri on a reduced volume allocation during the transition period.

**Scenario B**: The refrigeration reliability issue with PT Sentosa Cold Chain Logistics recurs in Q2 2026 despite the scheduled service review. Per Rule 2, this triggers an immediate root-cause investigation rather than waiting for the next quarterly report, given the food safety risk implications of repeated cold chain temperature excursions.
