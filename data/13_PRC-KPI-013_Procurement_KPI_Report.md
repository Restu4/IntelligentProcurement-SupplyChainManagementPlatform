## Purpose

This report consolidates the Procurement function's Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for FY2025 against Company targets, providing senior management and the Procurement Steering Committee with a comprehensive performance view across cost, compliance, quality, and supplier management dimensions.

### 2. Cost Savings Breakdown
Total realized savings of Rp 168.5 billion were achieved through: negotiated price reductions (Rp 71.2 billion, 42%), volume consolidation across plants (Rp 44.8 billion, 27%), specification optimization/value engineering (e.g., packaging gauge reduction with PT Wira Packaging Solutions, Rp 28.6 billion, 17%), and payment terms optimization/working capital benefit (Rp 23.9 billion, 14%). Savings are validated by Finance using the Company's Cost Avoidance vs. Realized Savings methodology, with only realized (budget-impacting) savings counted toward the headline figure.

### 3. OTIF Performance Analysis
The shortfall against the 95% OTIF target (94.1% actual) was driven primarily by the Packaging category (PT Karton Pak Nusantara and PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia underperformance, detailed in PRC-VPR-004) and a one-off logistics disruption in Q3 2025 affecting deliveries to the Surabaya plant during a port labor action at Tanjung Perak. Raw material category OTIF remained strong at 96.8%.

### 4. Maverick Spend Analysis
Off-contract ("maverick") spend of 6.8% exceeded the 5% target, concentrated in indirect categories: facilities/MRO (Rp 84,600,000,000), IT hardware (Rp 41,200,000,000), and ad hoc marketing/promotional materials (Rp 51,300,000,000). Root cause analysis attributes this to insufficient catalog coverage in SAP Ariba for long-tail indirect categories and business units bypassing Procurement for low-value, perceived-urgent purchases. A Catalog Expansion Initiative is planned for H1 2026 to bring an additional 1,200 SKUs under managed contract.

### 5. Procurement Cycle Time
Average PR-to-PO cycle time of 2.6 working days outperformed the 3-day target, reflecting the benefit of the SAP Ariba workflow automation rollout completed in Q2 2024 and the streamlined three-quote digital RFQ tool launched in Q1 2025.

### 6. Ethics Training Completion Gap
97.3% completion against the 100% target by 31 January 2026 deadline reflects 38 employees (out of approximately 1,410 in scope) who had not completed annual certification by the deadline, primarily due to extended leave or onboarding timing. Per PRC-PEP-011, system access for non-compliant employees was suspended pending completion; full completion was achieved by 15 February 2026.

## Rules

1. Cost savings must be validated by Finance before inclusion in the official KPI report; unvalidated "soft" savings (cost avoidance) are reported separately and not included in the headline savings percentage.
2. Any KPI missing target by more than 10% (relative) for two consecutive quarters requires a formal root-cause analysis and improvement plan presented to the Procurement Steering Committee.
3. Maverick spend by business unit is reported individually to the relevant Department Head monthly to drive accountability.

## Procedures

1. Procurement Analytics Unit extracts data monthly from SAP Ariba, SAP MM, and Finance GL systems.
2. KPIs calculated using standardized definitions per the Procurement KPI Dictionary (maintained separately).
3. Monthly KPI dashboard reviewed by Category Managers; quarterly consolidated report reviewed by Procurement Steering Committee.
4. Annual report finalized, validated by Finance, and presented to the Board Risk Committee.

## Thresholds

| KPI | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| OTIF | ≥95% | 90–94.9% | <90% |
| Maverick Spend | ≤5% | 5.1–8% | >8% |
| Quality Reject Rate | ≤1.5% | 1.6–2.5% | >2.5% |
| Ethics Training Completion | 100% | 95–99.9% | <95% |

## Penalties

- Sustained Red-rated KPI for two consecutive quarters without an approved improvement plan results in the relevant Category Manager being placed on a Performance Improvement Plan by the Supply Chain Director.
- Department heads with Maverick Spend exceeding 10% of their budget face budget approval restrictions until a remediation plan is agreed with Procurement.

## Approval Levels

The annual KPI report is approved by the Supply Chain Director and noted by the Board Risk Committee. Category-level improvement plans arising from Amber/Red ratings require Senior Procurement Manager sign-off; Company-wide initiatives (e.g., Catalog Expansion Initiative) require Procurement Director approval and budget allocation via the Budget Control Policy (PRC-BCP-017).

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: Given the OTIF shortfall is concentrated in Packaging, the Procurement Steering Committee approves a targeted FY2026 OTIF Recovery Plan focused on PT Karton Pak Nusantara and PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia, with monthly tracking against the 95% target rather than waiting for the standard quarterly cycle.

**Scenario B**: The Catalog Expansion Initiative to address Maverick Spend is budgeted at Rp 1,200,000,000 for system configuration and supplier catalog onboarding support. As this exceeds Rp 750,000,000, the initiative requires Procurement Director approval per PRC-PAM-008 before funding is released.
