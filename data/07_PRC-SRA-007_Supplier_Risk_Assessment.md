## Purpose

This report assesses supply continuity, financial, geopolitical, and compliance risk across PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia's top 12 raw material suppliers by spend, representing approximately Rp 1.6 trillion (42%) of total annual procurement spend. The assessment informs business continuity planning, dual-sourcing strategy, and insurance/hedging decisions.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Risk Assessment Methodology
Each supplier is scored across five risk dimensions on a 1 (low risk) to 5 (high risk) scale: Financial Stability, Single-Source Dependency, Geographic/Geopolitical Concentration, Regulatory & Compliance Exposure, and Operational/Quality Risk. A composite Risk Index is calculated (average of five dimensions); suppliers scoring 3.5 or above are classified "High Risk" and require a documented mitigation plan.

### 3. Critical Risk Findings

**Global Flavours & Fragrances Pte Ltd (Singapore)**: This supplier is the sole qualified source for three proprietary flavor compounds used in flagship instant noodle SKUs, representing a Single-Source Risk score of 5. Geographic risk is elevated due to reliance on a single manufacturing site in Johor, Malaysia, with no qualified backup facility. A supply disruption (e.g., regional flooding, port congestion at Tanjung Pelepas) could halt production of affected SKUs within 3 weeks based on current safety stock of 21 days.

**Asia Pacific Resin Trading Ltd**: Imported PET resin pricing is highly exposed to global crude oil volatility and Malaysian Ringgit/Rupiah exchange rate fluctuation. Operational risk is compounded by reliance on a single shipping route through the Malacca Strait, creating exposure to potential congestion or geopolitical disruption.

**PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia**: Operational risk score of 4 reflects the declining performance trend identified in the Q2 2026 Vendor Performance Report (PRC-VPR-004), including OTIF below SLA and recurring quality non-conformances, compounding financial risk concerns following a credit rating downgrade reported by a third-party financial intelligence provider in March 2026.

### 4. Mitigation Strategies
- **Global Flavours & Fragrances Pte Ltd**: Initiate qualification of an alternate flavor house (target: PT Bahan Baku Sejahtera's flavor division or an Indonesian-based formulator) within 6 months; increase safety stock to 35 days for the three critical SKU flavor compounds in the interim.
- **Asia Pacific Resin Trading Ltd**: Negotiate a hedging clause linking resin pricing to a published index with quarterly true-up rather than ad hoc surcharges; evaluate a secondary domestic resin distributor.
- **PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia**: Activate dual-sourcing per the Corrective Action Plan referenced in PRC-VPR-004; reduce dependency below 60% of category spend within 2 quarters.

## Rules

1. Any supplier with Single-Source Dependency risk score of 5 must have a documented qualification pathway for an alternate source within 12 months, regardless of current performance satisfaction.
2. Suppliers with a Composite Risk Index of 3.5 or above must be reviewed quarterly rather than the standard semi-annual cycle.
3. Any supplier representing more than 15% of a single raw material category's total spend triggers a mandatory concentration risk review by the Procurement Steering Committee.
4. Financial Risk score of 4 or 5 (indicating going-concern or severe liquidity concerns) requires immediate engagement with Treasury to assess advance payment exposure and potential need for credit insurance.

## Procedures

1. Procurement Risk Analyst compiles spend, performance, and external financial intelligence data quarterly.
2. Risk scoring workshop conducted with Category Managers to validate scores against operational reality.
3. Composite Risk Index calculated and risk register updated.
4. High-risk suppliers (≥3.5) require mitigation plan submission within 30 days.
5. Risk register and mitigation status presented to Procurement Steering Committee monthly; Board Risk Committee receives summary quarterly.

## Thresholds

| Composite Risk Index | Classification | Review Frequency | Action Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 – 1.9 | Low Risk | Annual | Standard monitoring |
| 2.0 – 2.9 | Moderate Risk | Semi-annual | Monitor trends |
| 3.0 – 3.4 | Elevated Risk | Quarterly | Mitigation plan recommended |
| 3.5 – 5.0 | High Risk | Quarterly, mandatory | Mitigation plan mandatory within 30 days |

## Penalties

- Failure by a Category Manager to submit a mitigation plan within 30 days for a High Risk supplier results in escalation to the Supply Chain Director and a formal note in the Category Manager's performance review.
- Continued reliance on a single-source supplier beyond 12 months without a documented and Board-acknowledged justification is flagged as a governance gap in the next Procurement Audit Report (PRC-PAR-009).

## Approval Levels

Risk register updates: Procurement Risk Analyst prepares, Category Manager validates, Supply Chain Director approves. High Risk classification and associated mitigation budget above Rp 750,000,000 (e.g., new supplier qualification costs) requires Procurement Director approval per PRC-PAM-008.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: A port congestion event at Tanjung Pelepas delays a shipment from Global Flavours & Fragrances Pte Ltd by 18 days. Because mitigation stock was raised to 35 days following this risk assessment's recommendation, production continuity is maintained without SKU disruption, validating the mitigation investment.

**Scenario B**: PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia's credit rating deteriorates further in Q3 2026. Per Rule 4, Treasury is engaged to reassess advance payment terms, and the Category Manager accelerates the dual-sourcing timeline with Asia Pacific Resin Trading Ltd, bringing forward the qualification audit originally scheduled for Q4 2026.
