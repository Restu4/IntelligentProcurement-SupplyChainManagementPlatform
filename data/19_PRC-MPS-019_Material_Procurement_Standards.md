## Purpose

This document establishes the technical and quality standards that all raw materials and packaging materials must meet before acceptance into PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia's production process, ensuring consistent finished product quality and regulatory compliance across all manufacturing sites.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Material Specification Sheets (MSS)
Every raw material and packaging item procured must have an approved Material Specification Sheet on file, jointly owned by Quality Assurance (technical parameters) and Procurement (commercial terms), covering: physical/chemical properties, acceptable tolerance ranges, packaging and labeling requirements, shelf-life/storage conditions, and required certifications (Halal, BPOM, ISO).

### 2. Incoming Quality Inspection (IQC) Standards
All incoming raw material and packaging shipments are subject to Incoming Quality Control inspection before Goods Receipt posting:
- **Critical materials** (e.g., food additives, flavor compounds): 100% Certificate of Analysis (CoA) review plus sample testing on every batch.
- **Major materials** (e.g., primary packaging film): CoA review on every batch plus sample testing on a statistical sampling basis (AQL 1.0, per ISO 2859-1).
- **Minor materials** (e.g., secondary cartons, labels): CoA review on every batch; physical sample testing on a risk-based frequency (minimum 1 in 10 batches).

### 3. Acceptance Quality Limits (AQL)
Defect tolerance for incoming materials follows ISO 2859-1 sampling standards: Critical defects (e.g., contamination, foreign material) AQL 0.065; Major defects (e.g., out-of-spec concentration, dimensional non-conformance) AQL 1.0; Minor defects (e.g., cosmetic packaging flaws) AQL 2.5.

### 4. Halal and Food Safety Compliance
All raw materials and food-contact packaging materials must carry valid Halal certification issued by BPJPH/MUI before acceptance. Materials lacking valid certification are automatically rejected at IQC regardless of technical conformance, and the shipment is placed on hold pending supplier remediation or replacement. All food additive suppliers must additionally hold current BPOM registration numbers, verified annually against the BPOM public registry.

### 5. Material Substitution and Change Control
Any supplier-initiated change to raw material composition, source location, or manufacturing process ("Notice of Change") must be submitted to Quality Assurance and Procurement at least 90 days before implementation. Changes affecting food safety, allergen status, or Halal status require re-validation testing and Quality Assurance approval before the changed material can be accepted; Procurement may not authorize commercial acceptance of a changed material ahead of Quality Assurance sign-off.

### 6. Shelf-Life and Storage Requirements
Materials must be received with a minimum remaining shelf life of 70% of total stated shelf life at the time of delivery (e.g., a material with 12-month shelf life must have at least 8.4 months remaining upon receipt) unless a documented exception is granted for fast-turnover materials with confirmed consumption plans.

### 7. Packaging Material Standards
Primary packaging (direct food contact) must comply with BPOM Regulation on food packaging migration limits and undergo migration testing for new packaging materials before commercial approval. Secondary and tertiary packaging (cartons, pallet wrap) must meet defined compression strength and moisture resistance standards appropriate to the Company's distribution environment (tropical humidity, multi-modal transport).

### 8. Non-Conforming Material (NCM) Handling
Materials failing IQC inspection are tagged as Non-Conforming Material, quarantined in a designated hold area, and dispositioned within 5 working days via one of: return to supplier, rework/sort (if feasible), use-with-deviation (requires Quality Assurance Manager approval and documented risk assessment), or scrap. NCM disposition decisions and associated costs are tracked against the responsible supplier's quality performance record feeding into PRC-SER-003.

## Rules

1. No raw material or packaging item may be used in production without an approved Material Specification Sheet and passed IQC inspection.
2. Critical material CoA review is mandatory on 100% of batches with no exception.
3. Materials failing Halal certification verification are automatically rejected regardless of any other quality parameter.
4. Supplier Notice of Change requirements apply to all Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers; non-compliance with the 90-day notice requirement is treated as a contractual breach.
5. Use-with-deviation dispositions require documented risk assessment and Quality Assurance Manager sign-off; Procurement alone cannot authorize use of non-conforming material.

## Procedures

1. Procurement issues PO referencing the approved Material Specification Sheet number.
2. Supplier ships material with accompanying CoA and Halal/BPOM documentation.
3. Warehouse receives shipment and notifies Quality Assurance for IQC inspection before Goods Receipt finalization.
4. QA performs CoA review and sample testing per category-based frequency.
5. Conforming material released for Goods Receipt and production use; non-conforming material tagged NCM and quarantined.
6. NCM disposition decided within 5 working days; outcome recorded in SAP QM and fed into supplier quality scorecard.

## Thresholds

| Material Class | CoA Review | Sample Testing Frequency | AQL (Major Defects) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical (additives, flavors) | 100% of batches | 100% of batches | 0.065 (critical defects) |
| Major (primary packaging, key raw materials) | 100% of batches | Statistical (AQL 1.0) | 1.0 |
| Minor (secondary packaging, labels) | 100% of batches | Risk-based (min. 1 in 10) | 2.5 |
| Minimum remaining shelf life at receipt | 70% of total shelf life | — | — |

## Penalties

- Acceptance of material into production without completed IQC inspection is a critical control violation, subject to immediate investigation by Quality Assurance and potential disciplinary action against the responsible Warehouse/Production supervisor.
- Repeated NCM incidents (3 or more in a rolling 6-month period) from a single supplier trigger mandatory Corrective Action Plan and contribute negatively to the supplier's quality sub-score in the next Supplier Evaluation Report.
- Falsified or altered CoA documentation discovered at any stage results in immediate supplier suspension and referral to Compliance & Ethics Office per PRC-PCP-006.

## Approval Levels

Material Specification Sheet approval: joint Quality Assurance and Category Manager sign-off. Use-with-deviation disposition: Quality Assurance Manager approval mandatory; for Critical materials, additional Head of Quality Assurance sign-off required. Supplier Notice of Change affecting Halal/allergen status: Head of Quality Assurance approval required before commercial acceptance.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: PT Sinar Kimia Abadi submits a Notice of Change indicating a shift in their citric acid manufacturing process to a new production line within the same facility. Quality Assurance determines the change does not affect food safety or Halal status after reviewing supporting documentation, and approves the change with standard (rather than full re-validation) verification, allowing Procurement to continue accepting material without supply disruption.

**Scenario B**: A shipment of flexible packaging film from PT Wira Packaging Solutions fails IQC sample testing due to print registration defects exceeding the AQL 1.0 threshold. The material is tagged NCM and quarantined; Quality Assurance determines a sort-and-rework disposition is feasible for 70% of the shipment, with the remaining 30% returned to supplier, and the incident is logged against PT Wira Packaging Solutions' quality record for the next quarterly evaluation.
