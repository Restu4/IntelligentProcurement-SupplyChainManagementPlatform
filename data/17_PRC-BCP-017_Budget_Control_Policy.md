## Purpose

This policy governs how procurement spend is budgeted, tracked, and controlled against approved annual budgets at PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia, ensuring financial discipline across all cost centers and preventing unauthorized budget overruns.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Annual Budget Cycle
The Company's annual procurement budget (FY2026: Rp 4.05 trillion, reflecting projected volume growth and input cost inflation) is built bottom-up from plant-level demand forecasts, validated by Category Managers, and consolidated by Finance into the Annual Operating Plan (AOP), approved by the Board in December for the following fiscal year.

### 2. Budget Categories
- **Direct Material Budget**: Raw materials and packaging tied directly to production volume (variable budget, flexes with actual production volume within ±10% before requiring review).
- **Indirect/MRO Budget**: Fixed annual allocation per cost center, reviewed quarterly.
- **Capital Expenditure (Capex) Budget**: Project-based, approved individually per the Capex approval matrix (PRC-PAM-008 Section 3).
- **Contingency Reserve**: 3% of total procurement budget held centrally for emergency procurement and unforeseen cost escalation, released only with CFO approval.

### 3. Budget Availability Check
Every Purchase Requisition in SAP Ariba triggers an automated budget availability check against the relevant cost center's remaining annual allocation. Requisitions that would not cause the cost center to exceed 100% of allocated budget proceed automatically to the standard approval workflow (PRC-PAM-008). Requisitions that would cause a cost center to exceed its budget require additional Finance Business Partner approval before routing to standard procurement approval.

### 4. Budget Variance Thresholds

| Variance vs. Annual Budget (Cost Center Level) | Required Action |
|---|---|
| Within ±5% | Standard monitoring, no additional approval |
| 5.1% – 10% over | Finance Business Partner approval required for further spend |
| 10.1% – 20% over | Finance Business Partner + Department Head approval; quarterly forecast re-baseline triggered |
| Above 20% over | CFO approval required; formal budget reforecast and Board Risk Committee notification if structural (not one-off) |

### 5. Direct Material Budget Flexing
Because direct material spend is volume-driven, the Direct Material Budget automatically flexes proportionally with actual production volume (e.g., if production volume is 8% above plan, the direct material budget ceiling increases by a corresponding 8%, subject to Finance validation of the volume driver). This flexing does not apply to indirect or Capex budgets.

### 6. Contingency Reserve Usage
Access to the centrally held Contingency Reserve (Rp 121,500,000,000 for FY2026, 3% of total budget) requires a Contingency Release Request submitted to Finance, documenting the unforeseen nature of the spend (e.g., emergency procurement following a Supply Chain Incident such as PRC-SCI-014) and CFO approval regardless of amount.

### 7. Mid-Year Budget Reallocation
Category Managers may request reallocation of unused budget from one category to another (e.g., shifting savings from favorable raw material pricing to fund an unplanned packaging cost increase) via a formal Budget Reallocation Request, approved by the Finance Business Partner for amounts up to Rp 500,000,000, and CFO for amounts above that.

### 8. Budget Reporting
Procurement Analytics Unit and Finance jointly publish a monthly Budget vs. Actual report by cost center and category, reviewed at the monthly Procurement Steering Committee meeting and the Finance Management Review.

## Rules

1. No Purchase Requisition may be approved that exceeds available budget without the corresponding Finance approval tier per Section 4.
2. Contingency Reserve funds may not be used for routine, foreseeable spend; misuse is treated as a budget control violation.
3. Category Managers must provide a written cost driver explanation for any cost center variance exceeding 10% within 5 working days of the monthly report.
4. Budget reallocation between Capex and operating expense categories is prohibited under Indonesian financial reporting standards (PSAK) and Company accounting policy; reallocation is only permitted within like categories (Capex-to-Capex, Opex-to-Opex).

## Procedures

1. Annual budget built bottom-up and approved by Board in Q4 of prior fiscal year.
2. Budget loaded into SAP by cost center, GL account, and category at start of fiscal year.
3. Every PR triggers automated budget availability check.
4. Variance reports generated monthly; out-of-tolerance variances trigger the approval escalation per Section 4.
5. Contingency Reserve and reallocation requests processed via Finance's Budget Exception workflow.
6. Quarterly reforecast conducted to true-up the Direct Material Budget against actual production volume.

## Penalties

- Repeated submission of PRs exceeding budget without proper Finance approval results in suspension of the Category Manager's PR creation rights pending retraining.
- Use of Contingency Reserve funds for foreseeable, routine spend without proper justification is treated as a financial control violation, reported to Internal Audit, and may require repayment from the originating cost center's future budget.
- Failure to provide variance explanations within the 5-working-day requirement results in automatic escalation of the variance to the Procurement Director for direct follow-up.

## Approval Levels

Budget availability exceptions follow Section 4. Contingency Reserve release: CFO approval mandatory regardless of amount. Mid-year reallocation: Finance Business Partner up to Rp 500,000,000; CFO above that amount. Annual budget itself: Board of Directors approval.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: Following the Cikarang citric acid Supply Chain Incident (PRC-SCI-014), the emergency air-supported freight cost of Rp 186,000,000 is funded from the Contingency Reserve via a Contingency Release Request, approved by the CFO given the clearly unforeseen, non-routine nature of the expense.

**Scenario B**: The Packaging category cost center is tracking 14% over its annual budget by Q3 2026 due to resin price inflation. This falls in the 10.1–20% band, requiring both Finance Business Partner and Department Head approval for any further packaging spend, plus a formal quarterly forecast re-baseline to determine whether the overage is structural (likely requiring CFO-level budget reforecast) or a temporary timing issue.
