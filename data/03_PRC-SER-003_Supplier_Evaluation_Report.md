## Purpose

This report documents the formal periodic evaluation of PT Sinar Kimia Abadi ("PSKA"), a Tier-1 supplier of food-grade citric acid, sodium benzoate, and emulsifiers to PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia's Cikarang and Surabaya plants. The evaluation determines continued eligibility for the Preferred Supplier List (PRC-PSL-015) and informs contract renewal negotiations.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Evaluation Methodology
PSKA was evaluated using the Company's standard Supplier Scorecard, weighted as follows: Quality (25%), Delivery Reliability (20%), Price Competitiveness (20%), Compliance & Documentation (15%), Responsiveness & Service (10%), Sustainability (10%). Scores are derived from SAP Quality Module rejection data, on-time-in-full (OTIF) delivery logs, invoice price variance reports, and Compliance & Ethics Office screening records.

### 2. Quality Performance
PSKA recorded a batch rejection rate of 0.6% across 212 incoming shipments in the evaluation period, against the Company's acceptable threshold of ≤1.5%. Two minor non-conformances were logged: one related to incorrect Certificate of Analysis (CoA) documentation (resolved within 48 hours) and one related to packaging integrity on a single pallet (resolved via replacement at no cost). Quality score: 23.5/25.

### 3. Delivery Reliability
On-Time-In-Full (OTIF) performance was 96.2% against the contractual SLA target of 95%. Average lead time was 9.4 days against an agreed standard of 10 days. One delayed shipment occurred in December 2025 due to a force majeure event (flooding affecting the Tangerang industrial estate), which PSKA proactively communicated 5 days in advance and mitigated via air freight for the critical portion of the order at PSKA's own cost. Delivery score: 19/20.

### 4. Price Competitiveness
PSKA's pricing for food-grade citric acid (Rp 28,400/kg) was benchmarked against two alternate suppliers (PT Bahan Baku Sejahtera at Rp 29,100/kg and an importer quote at Rp 31,200/kg landed cost). PSKA remains within the top quartile of competitiveness, though a 2.1% price increase was implemented in January 2026 citing global feedstock cost inflation, which was reviewed and accepted by the Category Manager after benchmarking. Price score: 17/20.

### 5. Compliance & Documentation
PSKA maintains valid Halal certification (MUI), ISO 22000 Food Safety Management certification, and BPOM registration for all supplied additives. All compliance documents are current in the Vendor Master and were re-verified during this evaluation cycle. Compliance score: 15/15.

### 6. Responsiveness & Service
PSKA's account management team responded to all 14 logged service requests within an average of 6 business hours, against an SLA target of 24 hours. Responsiveness score: 9.5/10.

### 7. Sustainability
PSKA has submitted its Sustainability Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ) confirming wastewater treatment compliance and a documented carbon reduction roadmap targeting 15% emissions reduction by 2028. No independent third-party audit has yet been conducted; this is flagged as an action item. Sustainability score: 8/10.

### Total Composite Score: 92/100 (Rating: "Strategic Partner – Tier 1")

## Rules

1. Suppliers scoring below 70/100 are placed on a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) with a 90-day remediation window.
2. Suppliers scoring above 90/100 for two consecutive evaluation cycles are eligible for "Strategic Partner" status and may be considered for co-investment or exclusivity discussions.
3. Any compliance documentation gap (expired certificate, missing CoA) automatically caps the Compliance sub-score at 50% regardless of other factors until remediated.
4. Evaluations must be conducted at minimum semi-annually for Tier-1 suppliers and annually for Tier-2/Tier-3 suppliers.

## Procedures

1. Category Manager extracts performance data from SAP QM, Ariba, and Compliance systems.
2. Scorecard is calculated and validated against source data by the Procurement Analytics team.
3. Draft report shared with supplier for factual accuracy review (5 working days).
4. Final report reviewed by Supply Chain Director.
5. Outcome communicated to supplier via formal Supplier Performance Review meeting.
6. Report filed in Vendor Master record and referenced in next contract renewal cycle.

## Thresholds

| Composite Score | Classification | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | Strategic Partner | Eligible for long-term contract, volume growth |
| 80–89 | Preferred Supplier | Maintain status, annual review |
| 70–79 | Approved Supplier | Monitor, semi-annual review |
| Below 70 | At Risk | Corrective Action Plan, possible suspension |

## Penalties

- Score below 70 for two consecutive cycles results in mandatory re-tender of the category and possible removal from the Preferred Supplier List.
- Falsified CoA or compliance documentation discovered during evaluation results in immediate escalation to the Compliance & Ethics Office and suspension of new POs pending investigation.

## Approval Levels

Evaluation outcomes scoring "Strategic Partner" or above require Supply Chain Director sign-off. Outcomes below 70 require joint sign-off from Supply Chain Director and Compliance & Ethics Office before any CAP is issued.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: PSKA's score of 92/100 supports the Category Manager's recommendation to extend the framework agreement by an additional 24 months at favorable volume-tiered pricing, to be presented to the Procurement Director for approval given the contract's total value exceeds Rp 750,000,000.

**Scenario B**: Had PSKA's Halal certification lapsed without renewal during the evaluation period, the Compliance sub-score would have been capped, likely reducing the composite score below 80 and triggering a mandatory Corrective Action Plan with a 90-day cure period before any new PO could be issued against the framework agreement.
