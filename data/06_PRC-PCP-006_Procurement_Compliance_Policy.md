## Purpose

This policy sets out the compliance requirements that govern all procurement activity at PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia to ensure adherence to Indonesian law, international trade regulations, anti-corruption standards, and Company integrity standards. It works in conjunction with the Procurement Ethics Policy (PRC-PEP-011) and Procurement Regulatory Compliance document (PRC-PRC-020).

## Detailed Content

### 1. Regulatory Foundation
PT NMI's procurement compliance framework is built on: Law No. 8/2010 on Prevention and Eradication of Money Laundering, Law No. 11/1980 on Bribery (Tindak Pidana Suap), the Indonesian Anti-Corruption Law (UU No. 31/1999 as amended by UU No. 20/2001), BPOM Regulation on food safety registration, BPJPH Halal Product Assurance Law (UU No. 33/2014), and customs regulations under Bea Cukai for imported raw materials.

### 2. Know-Your-Supplier (KYS) Screening
Every new supplier must undergo KYS screening before vendor master activation, comprising: (a) corporate legal entity verification (NIB, NPWP, Akta Pendirian), (b) beneficial ownership disclosure, (c) sanctions and watchlist screening (OFAC, UN, Indonesian PPATK list), (d) adverse media screening, and (e) financial health check via Dun & Bradstreet or equivalent. Suppliers with adverse findings are escalated to the Compliance & Ethics Office for a risk-based decision before onboarding proceeds.

### 3. Gifts, Entertainment, and Hospitality
Procurement staff may accept gifts from suppliers only if the value does not exceed Rp 500,000 per occasion and Rp 1,500,000 cumulative per supplier per year, and the gift must be declared in the Gift Disclosure Register within 5 working days. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (vouchers, gift cards) are prohibited regardless of value. Business entertainment must be reasonable, infrequent, and have a legitimate business purpose; any single event above Rp 2,000,000 per person requires pre-approval from the direct supervisor.

### 4. Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption
Procurement staff are strictly prohibited from soliciting, accepting, offering, or facilitating any bribe, kickback, or improper payment to or from a supplier, government official, or third party in connection with any procurement decision. This includes "facilitation payments" to expedite customs clearance or permits, which are explicitly banned under Company policy even where locally common practice might suggest otherwise.

### 5. Conflicts of Interest
All Procurement employees must complete an annual Conflict of Interest (COI) Declaration. Any employee with a financial interest, family relationship, or other personal connection to a current or prospective supplier must disclose this immediately and recuse themselves from any sourcing decision involving that supplier. Failure to disclose a known conflict is treated as a serious compliance breach.

### 6. Trade Compliance and Sanctions
Procurement of imported raw materials and equipment must be screened against international sanctions lists prior to PO issuance. Any supplier or shipping route involving sanctioned jurisdictions requires Legal and Compliance pre-clearance. All customs declarations (PIB - Pemberitahuan Impor Barang) must accurately reflect HS codes, value, and origin; under-declaration to reduce import duty is strictly prohibited.

### 7. Whistleblower Protection
Employees, suppliers, or third parties may report suspected procurement misconduct via the Company's confidential whistleblower channel ("NMI Speak-Up Line," operated by an independent third party, available 24/7 via telephone and web portal). Retaliation against good-faith reporters is itself a disciplinary offense.

### 8. Compliance Audits
The Compliance & Ethics Office conducts quarterly transaction sampling audits across all procurement categories, with a minimum sample size of 5% of transactions above Rp 150,000,000, to verify adherence to this policy and PRC-SOP-001.

## Rules

1. All new suppliers must complete KYS screening; no PO may be issued against a vendor master record pending KYS clearance.
2. Gift and entertainment limits per Section 3 apply Company-wide with no exceptions for seniority.
3. COI declarations must be refreshed annually and immediately upon any material change in personal circumstances.
4. Any employee aware of a compliance breach has a mandatory duty to report via the Speak-Up Line or to the Compliance & Ethics Office.
5. Sanctions screening must be re-run annually for all active suppliers and immediately upon any change in supplier ownership structure.

## Procedures

1. New supplier compliance pack submitted via Supplier Onboarding Portal.
2. Compliance & Ethics Office completes KYS screening within 10 working days.
3. Outcome (Clear / Enhanced Due Diligence / Reject) recorded in Vendor Master.
4. Gift disclosures logged in real time via the Gift Disclosure Register (mobile app accessible).
5. Quarterly compliance audit findings presented to the Procurement Steering Committee and Audit Committee.
6. Substantiated breaches referred to HR Disciplinary process and, where applicable, external legal counsel.

## Thresholds

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Gift value per occasion | Rp 500,000 |
| Gift cumulative per supplier per year | Rp 1,500,000 |
| Entertainment requiring pre-approval | Above Rp 2,000,000 per person |
| Compliance audit sample (transactions > Rp 150,000,000) | Minimum 5% quarterly |
| KYS screening turnaround | 10 working days |

## Penalties

- Acceptance of a bribe, kickback, or undisclosed benefit results in immediate termination, referral to KPK (Komisi Pemberantasan Korupsi) or Polri where criminal conduct is suspected, and potential civil claims for damages.
- Failure to disclose a conflict of interest resulting in supplier award: the award is voided, subject to re-tender, and the employee faces disciplinary action up to termination.
- Exceeding gift/entertainment limits without disclosure: formal written warning on first offense; termination on repeat offense.
- Sanctions list violation: immediate supplier suspension, mandatory legal review, and potential regulatory self-reporting.

## Approval Levels

KYS Enhanced Due Diligence escalations require Chief Compliance Officer approval. Any exception to the gift/entertainment policy requires written approval from both the Chief Compliance Officer and Supply Chain Director, documented and retained for audit purposes.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: A Buyer is offered a Rp 3,000,000 dinner invitation by a representative of PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia during contract renewal negotiations. Per Section 3, this exceeds the Rp 2,000,000 pre-approval threshold; the Buyer must decline or obtain prior written approval from their supervisor, and in either case must log the disclosure in the Gift Disclosure Register.

**Scenario B**: During KYS screening of a prospective new chemical supplier, the Compliance & Ethics Office discovers the beneficial owner is a close relative of a Senior Procurement Manager at PT NMI. This triggers a mandatory Conflict of Interest review; the Senior Procurement Manager must recuse themselves from any sourcing decision involving this supplier, and the matter is escalated to the Chief Compliance Officer for a final onboarding decision.
