## Purpose

This Service Level Agreement defines the performance obligations, measurement methodology, and remedies governing the supply of flexible laminate packaging film by PT Wira Packaging Solutions ("the Supplier") to PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia ("the Company") under Master Supply Agreement CMP-2025-0033.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Scope of Supply
The Supplier shall supply flexible laminate packaging film for instant noodle and snack SKUs to the Company's Cikarang and Surabaya manufacturing facilities, with an estimated annual volume of 4,200 metric tons and an annual contract value of approximately Rp 287,000,000,000, delivered under a Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI) model per PRC-IRP-012.

### 2. Service Level Metrics and Targets

| Metric | Target | Measurement Method | Reporting Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| On-Time-In-Full (OTIF) Delivery | ≥95% | (Deliveries on-time and complete) / Total deliveries | Monthly |
| Quality Reject Rate | ≤1.5% | Rejected batches / Total batches received | Monthly |
| VMI Stock Availability | ≥98% (no stockout at Company facility) | Days with adequate VMI stock / Total days | Monthly |
| Order Lead Time | ≤10 calendar days | PO issuance to delivery confirmation | Per order |
| Response Time to Quality Complaints | ≤24 hours (acknowledgment), ≤5 working days (resolution plan) | Timestamp tracking in SRM system | Per incident |
| Invoice Accuracy | ≥99% | Invoices matching PO/GR without dispute | Monthly |

### 3. Pricing Mechanism
Base pricing is fixed for the first 12 months at Rp 68,300 per kilogram for standard laminate film specification. From month 13 onward, pricing is adjusted quarterly per an agreed resin price index formula: 60% linked to the published Platts Asia polypropylene resin index, 40% fixed (covering conversion cost), with a maximum quarterly adjustment cap of ±4% to provide budget predictability for both parties.

### 4. Service Level Credits (Penalties)
- OTIF below 95% but at or above 90%: Service Level Credit of 1% of that month's invoice value per percentage point shortfall below 95%.
- OTIF below 90%: Service Level Credit of 2% of that month's invoice value per percentage point shortfall below 90%, in addition to the credit calculated for the 90-95% band, capped at a combined maximum of 10% of monthly invoice value.
- Quality Reject Rate above 1.5%: Service Level Credit of 0.5% of monthly invoice value per 0.5 percentage point above threshold, plus full cost of replacement material at Supplier's expense.
- VMI Stockout causing production stoppage: Service Level Credit equal to the Company's documented direct production loss, capped at Rp 500,000,000 per incident, in addition to standard OTIF credits.

### 5. Service Level Bonus (Incentive)
The Supplier is eligible for a performance bonus of 0.5% of quarterly invoice value if OTIF exceeds 98% and Quality Reject Rate remains below 0.5% for the full quarter, recognizing sustained excellence beyond contractual minimums.

### 6. Cure Period and Escalation
Any SLA breach triggers a formal notification within 5 working days. The Supplier has a 30-day cure period to present and begin implementing a corrective action plan. If the same metric breaches SLA for 3 consecutive months despite the cure period, the Company may, at its sole discretion: (a) reduce allocated volume by up to 30% to enable dual-sourcing, (b) terminate the affected product category from the agreement with 60 days' notice, or (c) terminate the entire agreement for material breach per the Contract Management Policy (PRC-CMP-005).

### 7. Business Continuity and Force Majeure
Force majeure events (natural disaster, government-mandated shutdown, widespread infrastructure failure) suspend SLA credit calculation for the affected period, provided the Supplier notifies the Company within 48 hours of the event and provides documentary substantiation within 10 working days, consistent with the precedent established in PRC-SCI-014.

### 8. Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
Both parties commit to a formal Quarterly Business Review covering SLA performance, the Vendor Performance Report (PRC-VPR-004), upcoming demand forecasts, and continuous improvement initiatives (e.g., film gauge optimization for cost and sustainability benefit per PRC-SPP-021).

## Rules

1. Service Level Credits are deducted from the following month's invoice and are not subject to the Supplier's discretionary acceptance; credits apply automatically based on measured performance.
2. The Supplier must proactively notify the Company of any anticipated SLA shortfall at least 5 working days in advance where foreseeable, to qualify for cooperative mitigation rather than only retrospective credit application.
3. Service Level Bonus and Service Level Credit calculations in the same period are netted; a Supplier cannot earn a bonus in a quarter where any individual month within that quarter breached a critical SLA metric.
4. Any amendment to the SLA metrics or targets requires Category Manager and Supplier joint sign-off, filed as a formal contract amendment per PRC-CMP-005.

## Procedures

1. Supplier submits monthly performance self-report; Company independently validates against SAP/SRM system data.
2. Discrepancies between Supplier self-report and Company data are reconciled within 5 working days.
3. Validated performance data used to calculate Service Level Credits/Bonus, applied to next invoice cycle.
4. Cure period and escalation procedures triggered automatically upon 2 consecutive months of any metric breach.
5. QBR conducted quarterly with formal minutes filed in the CLM system.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: In March 2026, PT Wira Packaging Solutions records OTIF of 92% due to a temporary capacity constraint. This falls in the 90-95% band, triggering a Service Level Credit of 3% of that month's invoice value (3 percentage points below 95% × 1%). The Supplier proactively notified the Category Manager 6 working days in advance, demonstrating good-faith cooperation that is noted favorably in the next Supplier Evaluation Report despite the credit application.

**Scenario B**: A VMI stockout at the Cikarang plant in August 2026 causes a 6-hour production stoppage with documented direct production loss of Rp 420,000,000. Per Section 4, the Supplier is liable for a Service Level Credit equal to this documented loss (within the Rp 500,000,000 cap), in addition to the standard OTIF-related credit for that month, both deducted from the Supplier's September invoice.
