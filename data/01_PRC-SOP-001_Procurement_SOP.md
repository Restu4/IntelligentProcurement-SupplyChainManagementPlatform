## Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the mandatory end-to-end process for all procurement activities at PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia ("PT NMI" or "the Company") across its manufacturing sites in Cikarang, Surabaya, and Medan. The SOP ensures procurement is conducted in a manner that is transparent, cost-effective, compliant with Indonesian law, and aligned with the Company's annual procurement budget of approximately Rp 3.8 trillion. It applies to all direct materials (raw materials, packaging) and indirect spend (MRO, capital expenditure, services).

## Scope

This SOP applies to all employees within the Procurement Department, Category Managers, Plant Procurement Officers, and any staff with delegated purchasing authority under the Procurement Approval Matrix (PRC-PAM-008). It covers raw material procurement, packaging procurement, indirect/MRO procurement, capital equipment, and third-party logistics contracting.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Procurement Lifecycle
The procurement lifecycle at PT NMI consists of seven stages: (1) Demand Planning, (2) Requisition, (3) Sourcing & RFQ/RFP, (4) Supplier Selection & Negotiation, (5) Purchase Order Issuance, (6) Receiving & Quality Inspection, (7) Invoice Matching & Payment. Each stage must be executed within SAP Ariba (the Company's e-procurement platform) to maintain a complete audit trail.

### 2. Demand Planning
Plant-level demand planners submit a rolling 13-week material requirement plan (MRP) to the central Procurement Department every Friday by 17:00 WIB. Category Managers consolidate plant-level demand to leverage volume discounts across Cikarang, Surabaya, and Medan facilities.

### 3. Requisition Process
All purchase requisitions (PR) must be raised in SAP Ariba with a valid cost center, GL account, and budget line reference. Requisitions below Rp 5,000,000 may use the Petty Cash Procurement process (see Section 6). Requisitions for new suppliers must reference an active vendor master record created per the Supplier Onboarding Guidelines (PRC-SOG-010).

### 4. Sourcing Methods
- **Single Source**: Permitted only for sole-source raw materials, OEM spare parts, or where prior Board exception has been granted.
- **RFQ (Request for Quotation)**: Mandatory for spend between Rp 50,000,000 and Rp 750,000,000. Minimum three (3) competitive quotations required.
- **RFP (Request for Proposal)**: Mandatory for spend above Rp 750,000,000, capital projects, or strategic multi-year contracts.
- **E-Auction**: Used for commoditized raw materials such as palm oil derivatives and corrugated packaging where price transparency benefits the Company.

### 5. Supplier Selection
Suppliers are evaluated using the weighted scorecard: Price (30%), Quality (25%), Delivery Reliability (20%), Compliance & Sustainability (15%), Financial Stability (10%). A minimum composite score of 70/100 is required for award. Selection decisions must be documented in the Sourcing Decision Memo and attached to the PR in SAP Ariba.

### 6. Petty Cash / Low-Value Procurement
Purchases under Rp 5,000,000 may bypass the RFQ process but must still be processed through three verbal quotes where practicable and recorded in the Petty Cash Procurement Log, reconciled weekly by the Plant Finance Officer.

### 7. Emergency Procurement
In cases of unplanned production stoppage (e.g., critical raw material shortage), the Plant Procurement Officer may initiate an Emergency Purchase Order without the standard three-quote requirement, subject to immediate notification to the Procurement Director within 24 hours and retroactive justification documentation within 5 working days.

## Rules

1. No purchase order shall be issued without an approved Purchase Requisition.
2. No employee may approve a PO where they are also the requesting party above their personal procurement authority limit (segregation of duties).
3. All contracts above Rp 750,000,000 require Legal & Contracts review prior to supplier signature.
4. Procurement staff must declare any conflict of interest annually and prior to any sourcing event involving a related party.
5. All suppliers must pass Know-Your-Supplier (KYS) screening before vendor master creation.

## Procedures

1. Demand Planner raises MRP → 2. Requisitioner creates PR in SAP Ariba → 3. Category Manager validates budget availability → 4. Sourcing event executed (RFQ/RFP/E-Auction) → 5. Sourcing Decision Memo approved per PRC-PAM-008 → 6. PO issued to supplier → 7. Goods Receipt (GR) created by Warehouse → 8. Quality Assurance inspection logged → 9. Three-way match (PO/GR/Invoice) performed by Accounts Payable → 10. Payment released per agreed payment terms.

## Thresholds

| Spend Band (IDR) | Sourcing Method | Minimum Quotes |
|---|---|---|
| Up to Rp 5,000,000 | Petty Cash Procurement | 1–3 verbal |
| Rp 5,000,001 – Rp 50,000,000 | Direct Quote | 2 written |
| Rp 50,000,001 – Rp 750,000,000 | RFQ | 3 written |
| Above Rp 750,000,000 | RFP / E-Auction | 3 formal proposals |

## Penalties

- Bypassing the RFQ/RFP threshold without Procurement Director waiver: written warning and mandatory retraining; repeat violation may result in termination per HR Disciplinary Policy.
- Issuing a PO without an approved PR: the PO is voided, and the issuing officer's procurement authority is suspended for 90 days pending investigation by Internal Audit.
- Undisclosed conflict of interest discovered post-award: contract is subject to immediate review by Compliance & Ethics Office and may be terminated for cause.

## Approval Levels

Refer to PRC-PAM-008 (Procurement Approval Matrix) for full delegation of authority. Summary: Buyer (up to Rp 25,000,000) → Procurement Manager (up to Rp 150,000,000) → Senior Procurement Manager (up to Rp 750,000,000) → Procurement Director (up to Rp 2,500,000,000) → CFO + Procurement Director (up to Rp 10,000,000,000) → CEO/Board (above Rp 10,000,000,000).

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: The Cikarang plant requires an urgent purchase of food-grade citric acid worth Rp 320,000,000 from PT Sinar Kimia Abadi due to a forecast shortfall. Because the amount exceeds Rp 50,000,000, the Buyer must run a formal RFQ with at least three suppliers (e.g., PT Sinar Kimia Abadi, PT Bahan Baku Sejahtera, and an alternate importer) unless an Emergency Procurement justification is filed and approved by the Procurement Director within 24 hours.

**Scenario B**: A Plant Procurement Officer in Surabaya wants to purchase spare parts worth Rp 4,200,000 for a packaging line from CV Berkat Logistik Indonesia. As this falls under the petty cash threshold, two verbal quotes are sufficient, and the transaction is logged in the Petty Cash Procurement Log for weekly reconciliation.
