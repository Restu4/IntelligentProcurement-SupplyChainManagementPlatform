## Purpose

This report maintains the official register of suppliers, agents, and intermediaries prohibited from engaging in any commercial activity with PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia due to substantiated integrity, quality, safety, or contractual violations. It ensures blacklisted entities cannot re-enter the supply base under the same or a related legal identity.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Blacklist Criteria
A supplier is added to the Vendor Blacklist following one or more of the following substantiated findings, confirmed through formal investigation: (a) bribery, kickback, or corruption involving PT NMI personnel, (b) fraudulent documentation (falsified certificates, CoAs, financial statements), (c) critical food safety violation resulting in product contamination risk, (d) confirmed use of child labor or forced labor in the supply chain, (e) repeated, uncured material breach of contract, or (f) confirmed presence on an international sanctions list.

### 3. Investigation and Substantiation Process
No entity is added to the Vendor Blacklist without a formal investigation conducted or supervised by the Compliance & Ethics Office, including: evidence collection, the entity's right of response (minimum 14 days to respond to allegations), and a documented investigation report. Findings are reviewed by a Blacklist Review Panel comprising the Chief Compliance Officer, Procurement Director, and General Counsel before final listing.

### 4. Effect of Blacklisting
Blacklisted entities are permanently flagged in the Vendor Master system to prevent reactivation. Any attempt to onboard a new legal entity that shares beneficial ownership, registered address, or key personnel with a blacklisted entity triggers an automatic Enhanced Due Diligence hold pending Compliance & Ethics Office review, in accordance with anti-circumvention controls.

### 5. Distinction from Supplier Risk Assessment
Blacklisting is distinct from the "At Risk" classification under PRC-SRA-007. At Risk suppliers remain eligible for business subject to mitigation; blacklisted entities are entirely prohibited from any future commercial engagement, with the limited exception of the conditional 24-month review pathway noted for certain contract-breach (non-integrity) cases.

### 6. Permanent vs. Reviewable Listings
Integrity-related findings (bribery, fraud, sanctions) result in permanent blacklisting with no review pathway. Performance/contract-breach related findings (without an integrity dimension) may include a conditional review pathway after a minimum 24-month period, subject to demonstrated remediation and a fresh KYS screening.

### 7. Reporting and Governance
The Vendor Blacklist Report is reviewed quarterly by the Procurement Steering Committee and Compliance & Ethics Office, with any new additions reported to the Audit Committee within 30 days.

## Rules

1. No business unit may engage a blacklisted entity under any circumstance, including through a subcontractor or intermediary relationship, without explicit Board-level exception (which has never been granted to date).
2. Procurement and Compliance must cross-check new supplier onboarding requests against the Blacklist Register and related-party screening before any KYS clearance is issued.
3. Blacklist entries arising from integrity violations are never eligible for review or removal.
4. Any employee found to have knowingly engaged a blacklisted entity, or assisted in circumventing the blacklist via a shell entity, is subject to immediate disciplinary action up to termination.

## Procedures

1. Suspected violation identified (via audit, whistleblower report, QA finding, or routine screening).
2. Compliance & Ethics Office opens formal investigation; entity notified and given right of response.
3. Investigation report compiled with evidence and findings.
4. Blacklist Review Panel (CCO, Procurement Director, General Counsel) reviews and votes on listing decision.
5. Approved listings entered into Vendor Master with permanent system flag.
6. Audit Committee notified within 30 days; quarterly register reviewed by Procurement Steering Committee.

## Thresholds

| Finding Type | Listing Outcome | Review Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Bribery/Corruption | Permanent Blacklist | None |
| Fraudulent Documentation | Permanent Blacklist | None |
| Sanctions List Match | Permanent Blacklist | None |
| Critical Food Safety Violation | Permanent Blacklist | None |
| Repeated Uncured Contract Breach (non-integrity) | Conditional Blacklist | 24 months, subject to remediation evidence |

## Penalties

- Engaging a blacklisted supplier: immediate contract/PO cancellation, formal disciplinary action against responsible employee(s), and Audit Committee notification.
- Attempting to circumvent the blacklist via a related shell entity: treated as a serious integrity violation, subject to termination and potential referral to law enforcement, and the new entity is immediately added to the blacklist register as well.

## Approval Levels

Blacklist additions require unanimous Blacklist Review Panel approval (Chief Compliance Officer, Procurement Director, General Counsel). Any proposed removal/review (only applicable to the conditional 24-month pathway) requires the same Panel's approval plus fresh Enhanced Due Diligence KYS clearance.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: A Buyer in the Surabaya plant inadvertently receives a quotation from a trading company later discovered to be majority-owned by the same individual behind the blacklisted CV Tunggal Jaya Perkasa. The system's beneficial ownership cross-check flags this during KYS screening, automatically placing the onboarding request on hold pending Compliance & Ethics Office review, preventing circumvention of the blacklist.

**Scenario B**: PT Karya Plastindo Abadi requests reconsideration in April 2028 (24 months after blacklisting), submitting evidence of new quality management investment and ISO recertification. The Blacklist Review Panel evaluates the submission and requires a fresh, full Enhanced Due Diligence KYS screening and a trial audit before any decision to lift the conditional blacklist status is made.
