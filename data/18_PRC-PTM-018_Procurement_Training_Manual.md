## Purpose

This manual provides the structured curriculum and reference material for onboarding and continuously developing Procurement staff at PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia, ensuring consistent application of Company policy, system proficiency, and professional procurement skills across all plants.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Training Tracks
- **Foundation Track** (all new Procurement hires, Week 1–4): Company procurement policy overview (PRC-SOP-001), SAP Ariba navigation, Procurement Ethics Policy (PRC-PEP-011) certification, Approval Matrix (PRC-PAM-008) familiarization.
- **Category Specialist Track** (Months 2–6): Category-specific market knowledge (e.g., palm oil derivatives market dynamics, resin pricing indices, flexible packaging technology), supplier negotiation skills, RFQ/RFP design.
- **Advanced Sourcing Track** (Senior Procurement Manager and above): Strategic sourcing methodology (PRC-SSG-025), total cost of ownership (TCO) analysis, contract negotiation and risk allocation, supplier relationship management.
- **Compliance & Ethics Refresher** (all Procurement staff, annual): Mandatory annual recertification per PRC-PEP-011, covering anti-bribery, conflicts of interest, gift/entertainment limits, and whistleblower channels.

### 2. Foundation Track Curriculum (20 Training Hours)

| Module | Hours | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Procurement Policy Overview | 4 | PRC-SOP-001, PRC-POG-002 walkthrough with case studies |
| SAP Ariba System Training | 6 | PR/PO creation, approval workflow, supplier catalog, three-way match |
| Procurement Ethics & Compliance | 4 | PRC-PEP-011, PRC-PCP-006, KYS screening process |
| Supplier Onboarding Process | 2 | PRC-SOG-010 walkthrough |
| Negotiation Fundamentals | 4 | Basic negotiation techniques, role-play exercises |

### 3. Certification Requirements
New Procurement hires must pass a written assessment (minimum passing score: 80%) covering policy knowledge and a practical SAP Ariba simulation exercise before being granted independent PR/PO creation authority. Until certified, new hires operate under direct supervision with all transactions co-reviewed by an assigned mentor.

### 4. Negotiation Skills Curriculum
Category Specialist Track negotiation training covers: preparation and target-setting (BATNA analysis), competitive bid evaluation beyond price (total cost of ownership), managing supplier price escalation requests, and documenting negotiation outcomes in the Sourcing Decision Memo. Role-play scenarios use realistic case studies, such as negotiating a resin price index clause with Asia Pacific Resin Trading Ltd or managing a cost-reduction initiative with PT Wira Packaging Solutions.

### 5. Category-Specific Knowledge Requirements
- **Raw Materials Specialists**: Must understand commodity price index tracking (CPO/palm oil reference prices via Bursa Malaysia Derivatives, sugar via ICE), seasonal supply patterns, and BPOM/Halal regulatory requirements.
- **Packaging Specialists**: Must understand resin/paper pulp pricing drivers, sustainable packaging trends (recyclability, light-weighting), and printing/converting technical specifications.
- **Logistics/Indirect Specialists**: Must understand freight rate benchmarking, fuel surcharge mechanisms, and warehouse/3PL contract structures.

### 6. System Proficiency Requirements
All Procurement staff must demonstrate proficiency in: SAP Ariba (PR/PO management), SAP MM (inventory and goods receipt visibility), SAP QM (quality data review), and the Procurement Analytics Dashboard (Power BI). Proficiency is assessed via a practical exercise at 90 days and annually thereafter.

### 7. Continuous Professional Development
Senior Procurement Manager and above are encouraged to pursue external certification (e.g., CIPS - Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply, or CPSM - Certified Professional in Supply Management), with Company-sponsored examination fees up to Rp 15,000,000 per employee per certification, subject to a 2-year service commitment post-certification.

## Rules

1. No new Procurement employee may independently approve or release a PO until Foundation Track certification is completed and passed.
2. Annual Ethics Refresher completion is mandatory for continued system access, per PRC-PEP-011.
3. Mentors assigned to new hires must themselves have at least 2 years of tenure and no active disciplinary record.
4. Category Specialist Track completion is a prerequisite for promotion to Senior Procurement Manager.

## Procedures

1. New hire enrolled in Foundation Track within first week of employment.
2. Mentor assigned for first 90 days; all transactions co-reviewed during this period.
3. Written assessment and SAP Ariba simulation conducted at end of Foundation Track.
4. Upon passing, independent PR/PO authority granted at the Buyer level (Rp 25,000,000 threshold) per PRC-PAM-008; higher authority levels require additional tenure and Category Specialist Track completion.
5. Annual Ethics Refresher scheduled and tracked by HR Learning & Development, with completion status reported to Compliance & Ethics Office.
6. Continuous Professional Development requests submitted via the L&D Portal with Department Head approval.

## Thresholds

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Foundation Track passing score | 80% |
| Foundation Track duration | 4 weeks |
| Mentor co-review period | First 90 days |
| Annual Ethics Refresher completion | 100% by 31 January |
| Certification fee sponsorship cap | Rp 15,000,000 per employee per certification |

## Penalties

- New hire failing the Foundation Track assessment twice is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan with HR involvement; continued failure may result in role reassignment or termination during probation.
- Granting independent PO authority to an uncertified employee is a control violation attributable to the supervising manager, subject to disciplinary review.
- Failure to complete annual Ethics Refresher results in automatic system access suspension per PRC-PEP-011.

## Approval Levels

Training curriculum changes: Head of Procurement Operations approval. Certification fee sponsorship: Department Head approval up to the Rp 15,000,000 cap; exceptions above the cap require Procurement Director approval.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: A new Buyer joins the Medan plant and completes the Foundation Track in 3 weeks, passing the written assessment at 88%. After the standard 90-day mentor co-review period concludes without issue, the Buyer is granted independent PR/PO authority up to Rp 25,000,000 per the Approval Matrix.

**Scenario B**: A Procurement Manager seeking promotion to Senior Procurement Manager has not yet completed the Category Specialist Track. Per Rule 4, the promotion is deferred until the employee completes the required curriculum, with a development plan agreed between the employee and Head of Procurement Operations to close the gap within 2 quarters.
