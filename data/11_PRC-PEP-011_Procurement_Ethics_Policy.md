## Purpose

This policy establishes the ethical standards that govern the conduct of every individual involved in procurement activity at PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia, whether employee, contractor, or agent. It reflects the Company's zero-tolerance position on corruption, fraud, and unethical commercial conduct, consistent with Indonesian law and the Company's Code of Business Conduct.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Core Ethical Principles
PT NMI's procurement function operates on four principles: **Integrity** (acting honestly in all supplier dealings), **Fairness** (giving all qualified suppliers equal opportunity to compete), **Transparency** (decisions are documented and auditable), and **Accountability** (individuals own the outcomes of their procurement decisions).

### 2. Prohibited Conduct
The following are strictly prohibited without exception:
- Soliciting or accepting bribes, kickbacks, or "commissions" from suppliers in exchange for business.
- Disclosing competitor pricing or bid information to any supplier during a competitive sourcing event.
- Awarding business based on personal relationships rather than documented evaluation criteria.
- Using Company position to extract personal favors, discounts, or services from suppliers for private benefit.
- Accepting employment offers, internships for family members, or other indirect benefits from suppliers currently engaged in active sourcing events with that individual.
- Retaliating against any employee who reports suspected unethical conduct in good faith.

### 3. Related Party Transactions
Any procurement transaction involving a supplier in which a PT NMI employee, their immediate family member, or a close associate holds an ownership interest, directorship, or other material financial interest is classified as a Related Party Transaction (RPT). All RPTs must be disclosed to the Compliance & Ethics Office before any sourcing activity begins and require Audit Committee notification regardless of value. RPTs above Rp 150,000,000 require Audit Committee pre-approval.

### 4. Fair Competition Among Suppliers
All suppliers participating in a competitive sourcing event must receive identical specifications, timelines, and evaluation criteria. Category Managers must not provide any supplier with advance information, additional time, or favorable treatment not extended to all participants. Bid information from one supplier must never be shared with a competing supplier ("bid shopping" is strictly prohibited).

### 5. Confidentiality
Procurement staff must protect confidential supplier information (pricing, technical proposals, financial data) and Company information (production volumes, cost targets, strategic sourcing plans) from unauthorized disclosure, both during and after their employment with PT NMI.

### 6. Use of Company Resources
Procurement staff must not use Company time, systems, or supplier relationships to conduct personal business, including personal purchases routed through Company supplier accounts to obtain discounted pricing, which is explicitly prohibited even where the individual pays personally.

### 7. Reporting Obligations
Any employee who becomes aware of actual or suspected violations of this policy must report immediately via the NMI Speak-Up Line, their direct supervisor, HR, or the Compliance & Ethics Office. Reports may be made anonymously. Investigations are conducted confidentially with findings reported to the Audit Committee for matters involving senior management.

### 8. Annual Certification
All Procurement, Finance, and Category Management staff must complete annual Ethics Policy training and sign a certification of understanding and compliance by 31 January each year. Non-completion by the deadline results in suspension of procurement system access until completed.

## Rules

1. Zero tolerance: any substantiated bribery or kickback finding results in immediate termination and referral to law enforcement (KPK/Polri) where criminal conduct is indicated.
2. RPTs must be disclosed before sourcing activity begins; retroactive disclosure after award does not cure the violation.
3. Procurement staff must recuse themselves entirely (not merely disclose) from any sourcing decision involving a related party.
4. Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment for a minimum of 24 months.
5. Annual ethics certification is mandatory; non-completion triggers automatic system access suspension.

## Procedures

1. Employee identifies potential conflict or ethical concern.
2. Disclosure submitted via COI Declaration Form or Speak-Up Line.
3. Compliance & Ethics Office assesses and determines required action (recusal, RPT approval pathway, investigation).
4. For RPTs above Rp 150,000,000, submission to Audit Committee for pre-approval before sourcing proceeds.
5. Substantiated violations referred to HR Disciplinary Committee with Compliance & Ethics Office findings.
6. Outcomes logged in the Ethics Case Register, reviewed quarterly by the Audit Committee.

## Thresholds

| Item | Threshold |
|---|---|
| RPT disclosure | Mandatory regardless of value |
| RPT requiring Audit Committee pre-approval | Above Rp 150,000,000 |
| Annual ethics certification deadline | 31 January |
| Confidentiality obligation post-employment | 24 months minimum |

## Penalties

- Bribery, kickbacks, or fraud: immediate termination, forfeiture of any bonus/incentive pending the case, referral to law enforcement, and potential civil recovery action.
- Undisclosed RPT resulting in supplier award: contract voided/re-tendered, employee subject to termination, and supplier subject to review for Vendor Blacklist consideration.
- Bid shopping or unfair competitive practice: formal disciplinary action, potential termination, and corrective re-tender of the affected sourcing event.
- Failure to complete annual certification: system access suspension until remediated; repeated failure noted in annual performance review.

## Approval Levels

RPT approvals above Rp 150,000,000 require Audit Committee approval. All substantiated ethics violations involving Procurement Director-level or above personnel require Board notification regardless of transaction value.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: A Category Manager's spouse is appointed as a sales director at PT Bahan Baku Sejahtera, an existing approved supplier. The Category Manager must immediately disclose this RPT to the Compliance & Ethics Office and recuse themselves from all future sourcing decisions involving PT Bahan Baku Sejahtera; another Category Manager is assigned to manage this supplier relationship going forward.

**Scenario B**: During an RFQ process for cold chain logistics services, a Buyer mistakenly forwards PT Sentosa Cold Chain Logistics' pricing proposal to a competing bidder, CV Berkat Logistik Indonesia, while attempting to request a clarification. This constitutes a bid-shopping violation; the sourcing event must be voided and re-run with a fresh RFQ, and the incident is logged and reviewed by the Compliance & Ethics Office regardless of intent.
