## Purpose

This policy embeds environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations into PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia's procurement decisions, supporting the Company's public commitment to achieve a 30% reduction in Scope 3 supply chain emissions by 2030 (baseline year 2023) and to source 100% of palm oil derivatives from RSPO-certified sustainable sources by 2028.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Sustainable Sourcing Commitments
- **Palm Oil & Derivatives**: 100% RSPO (Roundtable on Sustainable Palm Oil) certified sourcing by 2028; current FY2025 achievement is 68%, sourced primarily through PT Agro Lestari Nusantara's RSPO-certified mills. No-Deforestation, No-Peat, No-Exploitation (NDPE) compliance is mandatory for all palm oil suppliers.
- **Packaging**: Target of 50% recycled content across plastic packaging by 2027 and 100% recyclable or compostable primary packaging by 2030, driving ongoing specification work with PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia and PT Wira Packaging Solutions.
- **Paper & Cartons**: 100% FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) or equivalent certified sourcing for all corrugated cartons, currently achieved through PT Karton Pak Nusantara.

### 2. Supplier Sustainability Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ)
All Tier 1 suppliers must complete an annual Sustainability SAQ covering: greenhouse gas emissions reporting (Scope 1 and 2 at minimum), water usage and wastewater treatment, waste management practices, labor and human rights standards, and supplier diversity (local/SME sourcing where applicable). SAQ responses feed into the Sustainability sub-score of the standard Supplier Scorecard (PRC-SER-003), weighted at 10% of total composite score.

### 3. Supplier Code of Conduct
All suppliers must formally acknowledge and comply with the PT NMI Supplier Code of Conduct, covering: prohibition of forced and child labor, fair wages at or above regional minimum wage (UMP/UMK), freedom of association, non-discrimination, environmental compliance, and anti-corruption standards consistent with PRC-PCP-006. Acknowledgment is a mandatory onboarding requirement per PRC-SOG-010.

### 4. Third-Party Sustainability Audits
Tier 1 suppliers in high ESG-risk categories (palm oil, chemicals, packaging manufacturing) are subject to third-party sustainability audits (e.g., SMETA - Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit, or equivalent) at minimum every 24 months, coordinated by the Sustainability & ESG Office with cost shared between PT NMI and the supplier per contractual agreement.

### 5. Carbon Footprint Data Collection
Category Managers must collect supplier-level carbon emissions data (or estimated emissions using recognized factors where supplier data is unavailable) for inclusion in the Company's annual Scope 3 emissions inventory, disclosed in the Sustainability Report and subject to limited external assurance.

### 6. Green Procurement Preference
Where two or more suppliers are otherwise commercially and technically comparable in a competitive sourcing event, preference is given to the supplier with superior sustainability credentials (certified sustainable sourcing, lower carbon intensity, recycled content), formalized as a tie-breaker criterion within the standard Supplier Scorecard methodology.

### 7. Sustainability-Linked Contract Clauses
Framework agreements with Tier 1 suppliers in palm oil and packaging categories increasingly incorporate sustainability-linked clauses, such as: progressive RSPO certification milestones, recycled content percentage commitments with defined ramp-up schedules, and audit cooperation obligations, with non-compliance treated as a material contract term subject to the standard contract breach remedies in PRC-CMP-005.

### 8. Local/SME Sourcing
PT NMI maintains a target that at least 30% of Tier 2/Tier 3 indirect spend (MRO, local services) is directed to qualified small and medium enterprises (UMKM) within the regions surrounding its manufacturing plants (Cikarang, Surabaya, Medan), supporting local economic development objectives tracked in the annual Sustainability Report.

## Rules

1. Tier 1 suppliers in palm oil, chemicals, and packaging categories must complete the annual Sustainability SAQ; failure to complete results in a capped Sustainability sub-score (maximum 3/10) until remediated.
2. Suppliers found in substantiated violation of the Supplier Code of Conduct (e.g., confirmed forced labor finding) are immediately escalated to the Vendor Blacklist process (PRC-VBL-016), regardless of commercial performance.
3. New palm oil supplier onboarding requires confirmed RSPO certification or a credible, time-bound certification pathway approved by the Sustainability & ESG Office.
4. Sustainability-linked contract milestones missed for two consecutive review periods trigger mandatory supplier engagement and may affect contract renewal terms.

## Procedures

1. Sustainability & ESG Office issues annual SAQ to all Tier 1 suppliers via the Supplier Onboarding Portal.
2. Responses validated against available third-party certifications and audit reports.
3. Sustainability sub-scores calculated and fed into the Supplier Scorecard (PRC-SER-003) by Category Managers.
4. Third-party audits scheduled and coordinated for high-risk category suppliers on the 24-month cycle.
5. Annual Scope 3 emissions data consolidated and reported in the Company Sustainability Report.
6. Non-compliance findings escalated per Rule 2 to Compliance & Ethics Office for Code of Conduct violations.

## Thresholds

| Commitment | Current Status (FY2025) | Target |
|---|---|---|
| RSPO-certified palm oil sourcing | 68% | 100% by 2028 |
| Recycled content in plastic packaging | 31% | 50% by 2027 |
| FSC-certified paper/carton sourcing | 94% | 100% by 2026 |
| Local/SME indirect spend share | 24% | 30% (ongoing) |
| Tier 1 supplier SAQ completion rate | 89% | 100% |

## Penalties

- Confirmed Supplier Code of Conduct violation involving forced/child labor: immediate Vendor Blacklist escalation, contract termination for cause, and public disclosure consideration per Company ESG governance protocol.
- Failure to complete annual SAQ: capped Sustainability sub-score affecting overall composite score and potential PSL status downgrade.
- Missed sustainability-linked contract milestones: documented in the supplier's performance record and considered in contract renewal negotiations; repeated failure may result in reduced volume allocation in favor of better-performing suppliers.

## Approval Levels

SAQ scoring methodology changes: Head of Sustainability & ESG approval. Third-party audit scheduling and cost-sharing agreements: Category Manager and Sustainability & ESG Office joint approval. Code of Conduct violation escalation to Blacklist: per PRC-VBL-016 Blacklist Review Panel process.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: PT Agro Lestari Nusantara reports progress to 75% RSPO-certified volume in its FY2026 SAQ submission, ahead of the Company's blended target trajectory. The Sustainability & ESG Office recognizes this in the supplier's Sustainability sub-score and highlights the achievement in the Company's annual Sustainability Report as a supply chain progress milestone.

**Scenario B**: A third-party SMETA audit of PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia's manufacturing facility identifies inadequate wastewater treatment documentation. This is logged as a non-critical finding requiring a 90-day corrective action plan; persistent non-resolution beyond two audit cycles would escalate to a formal Code of Conduct compliance review, separate from but coordinated with the ongoing performance concerns already flagged in PRC-SRA-007.
