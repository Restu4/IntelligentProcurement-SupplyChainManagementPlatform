## Purpose

This report documents realized and pipeline cost optimization initiatives across PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia's procurement categories, supporting the Company's broader profitability improvement program and providing transparency to the Procurement Steering Committee on savings methodology and validation.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Cost Optimization Framework
Cost optimization initiatives are classified into five levers: (1) **Price Negotiation** (re-negotiating unit price without scope change), (2) **Volume Consolidation** (aggregating demand across plants for better pricing tiers), (3) **Specification/Value Engineering** (modifying technical specifications to reduce cost without compromising quality or safety), (4) **Supply Base Optimization** (re-tendering or dual-sourcing to increase competitive tension), and (5) **Payment Terms Optimization** (extending terms to improve working capital, validated jointly with Treasury).

### 3. Key Initiative Highlights

**Packaging Film Gauge Reduction (PT Wira Packaging Solutions)**: Through joint engineering review, laminate film thickness for snack packaging was reduced from 65 micron to 58 micron without compromising barrier properties or shelf-life performance, validated through 6 months of accelerated shelf-life testing by Quality Assurance. Annualized savings: Rp 19,400,000,000, with the added sustainability benefit of reduced plastic consumption aligned with PRC-SPP-021 targets.

**Volume Consolidation – Corrugated Cartons**: Previously independent carton procurement at Cikarang, Surabaya, and Medan plants was consolidated into a single national framework agreement with PT Karton Pak Nusantara, achieving a 7.2% blended price reduction through improved volume leverage (combined annual volume of 38,000 metric tons vs. fragmented plant-level purchasing). Annualized savings: Rp 31,200,000,000.

**Payment Terms Extension – Top 15 Suppliers**: Strategic Sourcing, in coordination with Treasury, renegotiated payment terms from Net 30 to Net 45 days with 11 of the top 15 suppliers by spend, generating a working capital benefit valued at Rp 23,900,000,000 (calculated using the Company's weighted average cost of capital of 9.2%).

**Citric Acid Dual-Sourcing Competitive Tension**: Following qualification of PT Bahan Baku Sejahtera as an alternate citric acid source (accelerated after the Cikarang incident documented in PRC-SCI-014), renewed competitive tension in negotiations with PT Sinar Kimia Abadi resulted in a 3.1% price reduction at the most recent contract renewal point, despite global feedstock cost inflation pressures. Annualized savings: Rp 9,800,000,000.

### 5. Savings Validation Methodology
All claimed savings must be validated by Finance Business Partners against a documented baseline (prior year price, prior specification cost, or a competitive benchmark) before inclusion in the official savings tracker feeding PRC-KPI-013. "Cost avoidance" (e.g., avoiding a price increase that would otherwise have applied) is tracked separately from "realized savings" (actual reduction versus prior period spend) to maintain credibility of the headline savings figure.

### 6. Risk Considerations in Cost Optimization
The Strategic Sourcing Unit explicitly screens all proposed initiatives against quality, food safety, and supply continuity risk before implementation. Specification changes affecting food-contact materials require Quality Assurance sign-off per PRC-MPS-019 before any savings can be realized, ensuring cost reduction never compromises product safety or compliance.

## Rules

1. No savings initiative affecting food safety, Halal status, or regulatory compliance may proceed without Quality Assurance and, where relevant, Legal & Regulatory Affairs sign-off.
2. Claimed savings must be validated by Finance before being counted toward official Procurement KPI reporting (PRC-KPI-013).
3. Volume consolidation initiatives spanning multiple plants require Supply Chain Director approval given cross-plant operational coordination implications.
4. Payment terms extensions must be assessed for supplier financial health impact (per PRC-SRA-007) to avoid creating undue financial stress on smaller suppliers that could increase supply continuity risk.

## Procedures

1. Strategic Sourcing Unit identifies and prioritizes cost optimization opportunities via category spend analysis.
2. Initiative business case developed, including risk assessment and required cross-functional sign-offs.
3. Pilot/validation phase conducted where applicable (e.g., shelf-life testing for specification changes).
4. Finance validates savings methodology and baseline before implementation.
5. Initiative implemented via contract amendment or new sourcing event.
6. Realized savings tracked monthly and reconciled quarterly against the pipeline forecast.

## Thresholds

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Specification change validation period (food-contact materials) | Minimum 6 months accelerated testing |
| Finance validation requirement | Mandatory before KPI inclusion |
| Cross-plant consolidation approval | Supply Chain Director |
| Payment terms extension financial health screening | Mandatory via PRC-SRA-007 methodology |

## Penalties

- Inclusion of unvalidated savings in official reporting is treated as a data integrity issue, subject to correction and review by Internal Audit if discovered post-publication.
- Implementing a specification change affecting food safety without Quality Assurance sign-off is a critical control violation per PRC-MPS-019, subject to immediate reversal and disciplinary review.

## Approval Levels

Individual initiatives below Rp 750,000,000 annualized impact: Category Manager approval with Finance validation. Initiatives above Rp 750,000,000: Procurement Director approval. Cross-plant consolidation initiatives: Supply Chain Director approval regardless of value, given operational complexity.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: The packaging film gauge reduction initiative requires 6 months of accelerated shelf-life testing before implementation. Despite pressure to realize savings faster, Quality Assurance holds firm on the full testing protocol per PRC-MPS-019, and the savings are only counted in the official tracker once testing confirms no shelf-life or barrier performance degradation, preserving the integrity of both the savings claim and product quality.

**Scenario B**: The proposed payment terms extension from Net 30 to Net 45 with a smaller Tier 3 supplier is screened against that supplier's financial health data, revealing limited working capital buffer. Per Rule 4, the Strategic Sourcing Unit excludes this supplier from the payment terms initiative to avoid creating supply continuity risk, focusing the initiative only on financially robust Tier 1 suppliers.
