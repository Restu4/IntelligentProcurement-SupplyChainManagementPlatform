## Purpose

This document defines the standardized process for evaluating, registering, and activating new suppliers in PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia's Vendor Master, ensuring all onboarded suppliers meet the Company's legal, financial, quality, and compliance standards before any procurement activity may commence.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Onboarding Triggers
Supplier onboarding is initiated when: (a) a Category Manager identifies a new sourcing requirement not covered by existing Preferred Suppliers, (b) a dual-sourcing or risk mitigation need arises per PRC-SRA-007, or (c) a business unit requests a specific supplier for a unique technical requirement.

### 2. Onboarding Documentation Requirements
Prospective suppliers must submit, via the Supplier Onboarding Portal:
- Company legal documents: Akta Pendirian Perusahaan (Deed of Establishment), NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha), NPWP (Tax ID), SIUP/domisili where applicable.
- Bank account verification letter for payment setup.
- Quality certifications relevant to category (ISO 9001, ISO 22000, HACCP, Halal MUI/BPJPH certification for food-contact materials, BPOM registration for additives).
- Financial statements for the last 2 fiscal years (audited, if annual revenue exceeds Rp 50,000,000,000).
- Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption self-declaration and beneficial ownership disclosure.
- References from at least 2 existing commercial clients of comparable scale.
- Environmental, Health & Safety (K3) compliance documentation for suppliers providing on-site services.

### 3. Tiered Onboarding Pathway
- **Tier 1 (Strategic/Direct Material)**: Full onboarding pack, on-site factory audit by Quality Assurance, KYS Enhanced Due Diligence, sample testing (minimum 3 batches), and trial order before framework agreement.
- **Tier 2 (Indirect/MRO)**: Standard onboarding pack, desktop document review, standard KYS screening.
- **Tier 3 (Low-value/One-off)**: Simplified onboarding (legal and tax documents only) for cumulative annual spend expected below Rp 50,000,000.

### 4. Factory Audit Requirements (Tier 1)
For suppliers of direct food-contact raw materials or packaging, a Quality Assurance factory audit is mandatory before approval, assessing: GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) compliance, HACCP implementation, traceability systems, pest control programs, and worker hygiene practices. Minimum passing audit score: 80/100. Scores between 65–79 result in conditional approval with a 90-day corrective action requirement; below 65 results in automatic rejection.

### 5. Sample Testing and Trial Orders
New direct material suppliers must pass laboratory testing on a minimum of 3 consecutive production batches against Company specification sheets before being approved for commercial-scale supply. A trial order (typically 10–20% of anticipated regular order quantity) is placed and evaluated for quality and delivery performance before full framework agreement execution.

### 6. Vendor Master Activation
Upon successful completion of all required steps, the Vendor Master Management Unit activates the supplier record in SAP with a unique Supplier Code (format: VEN-[CATEGORY]-[SEQUENCE]), enabling PO issuance. Activation requires joint sign-off from Category Manager, Quality Assurance (for Tier 1), and Compliance & Ethics Office (KYS clearance).

### 7. Probationary Period
All newly onboarded Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers are subject to a 6-month probationary period during which performance is monitored monthly (rather than the standard quarterly/semi-annual cycle) and any single critical quality non-conformance or OTIF below 85% triggers immediate review of continued engagement.

## Rules

1. No PO may be issued against a supplier record that has not completed KYS clearance, regardless of business urgency.
2. Factory audits for Tier 1 suppliers must be conducted by a qualified Quality Assurance auditor independent of the Category Manager proposing the supplier, to avoid bias.
3. Suppliers providing food-contact materials must hold valid Halal certification before any commercial order is placed, in compliance with UU No. 33/2014.
4. Existing suppliers seeking to supply a new product category must undergo re-onboarding assessment specific to that category; prior approval for one category does not extend automatically to others.
5. Onboarding documentation must be refreshed every 24 months or immediately upon expiry of any individual certificate.

## Procedures

1. Category Manager submits New Supplier Request Form with business justification.
2. Prospective supplier completes Supplier Onboarding Portal registration and document upload.
3. Compliance & Ethics Office performs KYS screening (10 working day SLA).
4. Quality Assurance schedules and conducts factory audit (Tier 1 only) within 30 days of document clearance.
5. Sample testing/trial order executed and evaluated against specification.
6. Vendor Master Management Unit activates Supplier Code upon all sign-offs received.
7. Probationary monitoring tracked monthly for first 6 months.
8. Full inclusion in standard performance review cycle (PRC-SER-003 methodology) after probation.

## Thresholds

| Tier | Annual Spend Expectation | Factory Audit | Sample Testing | KYS Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Above Rp 500,000,000 | Mandatory (min. score 80/100) | 3 batches minimum | Enhanced Due Diligence |
| Tier 2 | Rp 50,000,000 – Rp 500,000,000 | Not required | Risk-based, as needed | Standard |
| Tier 3 | Below Rp 50,000,000 | Not required | Not required | Basic legal/tax verification |

## Penalties

- Activating a Vendor Master record without completed KYS clearance is a control violation subject to disciplinary action against the Vendor Master Management Unit staff member responsible.
- Falsified or fraudulent onboarding documentation discovered post-activation results in immediate supplier suspension, referral to the Compliance & Ethics Office, and potential addition to the Vendor Blacklist (PRC-VBL-016).
- Failure to conduct the mandatory 6-month probationary monitoring is recorded as a process non-conformance in the next Procurement Audit.

## Approval Levels

Tier 1 onboarding: Category Manager + Quality Assurance Manager + Compliance & Ethics Office (all three required). Tier 2: Category Manager + Compliance & Ethics Office. Tier 3: Category Manager only, with system-automated basic KYS check.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: PT Mitra Label Indonesia is proposed as a new Tier 2 supplier of product labels for the Surabaya plant. The Category Manager submits the request; standard KYS screening clears within 8 working days; no factory audit is required, and the Vendor Master is activated within 12 working days of initial submission, beginning the 6-month probationary monitoring period.

**Scenario B**: A prospective Tier 1 supplier of preservative additives scores 71/100 on the Quality Assurance factory audit, falling in the conditional approval band. The supplier is granted conditional approval with a 90-day corrective action plan addressing pest control deficiencies identified during the audit; no commercial order may be placed until a re-audit confirms a score of at least 80/100.
