## Purpose

This document maintains the official register of suppliers approved for ongoing and new business at PT Nusantara Manufacturing Indonesia, based on sustained performance evaluation (per PRC-SER-003), risk assessment (per PRC-SRA-007), and compliance clearance (per PRC-PCP-006). Buyers and Category Managers must source preferentially from this list unless a documented exception is approved.

## Detailed Content

### 1. Listing Criteria
Inclusion on the PSL requires: (a) minimum composite Supplier Scorecard of 80/100 in the most recent evaluation, (b) active KYS compliance clearance with no open Enhanced Due Diligence flags, (c) valid quality/regulatory certifications appropriate to category, and (d) no unresolved Corrective Action Plan older than 90 days.

### 3. Status Definitions
- **Strategic Partner**: Score ≥90 for two consecutive cycles; eligible for co-investment, exclusivity discussions, and joint innovation projects.
- **Preferred**: Score 80–89; standard preferred sourcing status.
- **Approved**: Score 70–79; usable but not preferred first choice; monitored more closely.
- **Active CAP**: Currently under a Corrective Action Plan; usable for existing committed volume but new business expansion is restricted pending CAP closure.
- **High Risk – Single Source**: Flagged per Supplier Risk Assessment; mandatory dual-sourcing development required even while status remains Preferred.

### 4. Exception Sourcing
Buyers may source from a non-PSL supplier only with documented justification (e.g., unique technical specification, geographic constraint, PSL supplier capacity shortfall) and Senior Procurement Manager approval. Exception sourcing above Rp 150,000,000 additionally requires Category Manager sign-off and is logged in the PSL Exception Register for quarterly review.

### 5. Removal Criteria
A supplier is removed from the PSL if: composite score falls below 70 for two consecutive cycles, KYS clearance lapses or Enhanced Due Diligence finding is unresolved beyond 60 days, a Corrective Action Plan remains open beyond 180 days without material improvement, or the supplier is added to the Vendor Blacklist (PRC-VBL-016) for any reason.

### 6. New Additions
Suppliers are added to the PSL only after successful completion of onboarding (PRC-SOG-010), a minimum 6-month probationary period, and a first formal Supplier Evaluation Report scoring 80 or above.

## Rules

1. Buyers must check the current PSL before initiating any new sourcing event; the list is the default sourcing reference.
2. PT Karton Pak Nusantara's "Active CAP" status restricts new business expansion until the Corrective Action Plan referenced in PRC-VPR-004 is formally closed.
3. Global Flavours & Fragrances Pte Ltd's "High Risk – Single Source" flag does not restrict current sourcing but mandates active dual-source development tracked by the Category Manager.
4. The PSL must be republished and redistributed to all Procurement staff within 5 working days of any status change for a Tier 1 supplier.

## Procedures

1. Category Managers submit updated scorecards following each Supplier Evaluation Report cycle.
2. Procurement Analytics Unit consolidates scores and cross-checks KYS/compliance status with Compliance & Ethics Office.
3. Draft PSL update reviewed at the Procurement Steering Committee monthly meeting.
4. Final PSL approved by Supply Chain Director and published via the Procurement intranet portal and SAP Ariba catalog flagging.
5. Buyers receive automated system guidance flagging non-PSL suppliers at PR creation.

## Thresholds

| Composite Score | PSL Status |
|---|---|
| 90–100 (2 consecutive cycles) | Strategic Partner |
| 80–89 | Preferred |
| 70–79 | Approved |
| Below 70 | Not Listed / Removed |

## Penalties

- Repeated unjustified sourcing from non-PSL suppliers without approved exception is logged as a control violation and reviewed in the employee's performance evaluation.
- Failure to flag a supplier for removal despite meeting removal criteria (e.g., score below 70 for two cycles) is a Category Manager accountability gap escalated to Supply Chain Director.

## Approval Levels

PSL additions and removals: Category Manager recommends, Supply Chain Director approves. Exception sourcing from non-PSL suppliers above Rp 150,000,000: Senior Procurement Manager and Category Manager joint approval.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: A new product launch requires a specialty stabilizer not currently supplied by any PSL supplier. The Category Manager identifies a capable non-PSL supplier, documents the technical justification, and obtains Senior Procurement Manager approval for exception sourcing while simultaneously initiating formal onboarding (PRC-SOG-010) to bring the new supplier onto the PSL for future business.

**Scenario B**: PT Karton Pak Nusantara successfully closes its Corrective Action Plan in Q3 2026 with OTIF restored to 96%. Following confirmation in the next Supplier Evaluation Report, the Category Manager recommends removing the "Active CAP" flag and restoring full preferred status, to be approved at the next Procurement Steering Committee meeting.
