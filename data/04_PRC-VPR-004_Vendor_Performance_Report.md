## Purpose

This report consolidates vendor performance data for the Packaging category covering three key suppliers — PT Wira Packaging Solutions (flexible film), PT Karton Pak Nusantara (corrugated cartons), and PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia (PET bottles/closures) — to inform quarterly business reviews, contract management decisions, and the Procurement KPI consolidation report (PRC-KPI-013).

## Detailed Content

### 1. Category Overview
Total packaging spend for Q2 2026 was Rp 312,700,000,000, representing 8.2% of total quarterly procurement spend (Rp 3.81 trillion annualized run rate). The packaging category supports production lines at Cikarang (primary), Surabaya, and Medan.

### 3. PT Wira Packaging Solutions
Delivered 97.8% OTIF against the SLA target of 95%, supplying flexible laminate film for snack and instant noodle SKUs. Quality reject rate of 0.4% remained well below the 1.5% acceptable ceiling. Negotiated a 1.2% cost reduction during the quarter through a raw material index-linked pricing clause. No escalations were logged. This supplier remains on the Preferred Supplier List with "Strategic Partner" classification.

### 4. PT Karton Pak Nusantara
OTIF performance declined from 95.1% in Q1 2026 to 91.3% in Q2 2026, falling below the 95% SLA target, primarily attributed to a machine breakdown at their Cikarang converting facility in May 2026 that caused an 11-day backlog. Quality reject rate of 1.8% slightly exceeds the 1.5% threshold, driven by inconsistent box compression strength on one SKU line. A Corrective Action Plan (CAP) has been issued with a 60-day remediation deadline (refer to escalation ref. CAP-2026-014).

### 5. PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia
This supplier's performance has deteriorated for two consecutive quarters. OTIF of 88.6% is materially below the 95% SLA target, and the quality reject rate of 2.6% breaches the contractual ceiling. Price variance of +3.1% versus budget, driven by unapproved resin surcharges not pre-communicated per contract clause 8.3, is also a contractual non-compliance. This supplier has been escalated to the Supplier Risk Assessment process (PRC-SRA-007) and is now classified "At Risk."

### 6. Root Cause Analysis
Common contributing factors identified across underperforming suppliers include: (a) capacity constraints during the Ramadan/Lebaran production surge period, (b) raw material (resin, kraft paper) global price volatility passed through without adequate advance notice, and (c) insufficient buffer stock at supplier-managed warehouses.

### 7. Corrective Actions
- PT Karton Pak Nusantara: Mandatory weekly performance call with Category Manager for 8 weeks; capacity recovery plan submitted and accepted; OTIF must return to ≥95% by end of Q3 2026 or contract moves to re-tender.
- PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia: Formal Cure Notice issued under contract clause 14.2; 30-day cure period; if unresolved, dual-sourcing activation with Asia Pacific Resin Trading Ltd as backup supplier will proceed.

## Rules

1. Any supplier falling below 90% OTIF for two consecutive quarters is automatically escalated to Supplier Risk Assessment.
2. Quality reject rates exceeding the contractual ceiling for two consecutive months trigger a mandatory Corrective Action Plan.
3. Unapproved surcharges or price pass-throughs without contractual notice provisions are treated as a contract compliance breach, not merely a commercial issue.
4. Vendor Performance Reports must be shared with the supplier within 10 working days of quarter-end and discussed in a formal Quarterly Business Review (QBR).

## Procedures

1. Category Manager extracts OTIF, quality, and price variance data from SAP QM and Ariba Supplier Performance module.
2. Scorecards calculated and validated.
3. Underperformance triggers automatic CAP workflow in the Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) system.
4. QBR scheduled with supplier leadership.
5. Report and CAP status logged against the Vendor Master and reviewed by Supply Chain Director monthly.

## Thresholds

| Metric | Target SLA | Breach Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| OTIF | ≥95% | <90% for 2 consecutive quarters → Risk Escalation |
| Quality Reject Rate | ≤1.5% | >1.5% for 2 consecutive months → CAP |
| Price Variance vs Budget | ±2% | >2% unexplained → Compliance Review |

## Penalties

- Persistent SLA breach beyond the cure period results in Service Level Credits per the Supplier SLA Agreement (PRC-SLA-023), typically 1% of monthly invoice value per percentage point of OTIF shortfall, capped at 10% of monthly invoice value.
- Confirmed unapproved surcharge breach may result in the surcharge amount being clawed back against future invoices and a formal warning letter recorded in the Vendor Master.
- Failure to remediate within the cure period authorizes Procurement to initiate re-tender or dual-sourcing without further negotiation.

## Approval Levels

CAP issuance: Category Manager initiates, Senior Procurement Manager approves. Re-tender or dual-sourcing activation due to non-remediation: requires Procurement Director approval. Contract termination for cause: requires Procurement Director and Legal & Contracts joint approval.

## Business Scenarios

**Scenario A**: If PT Cahaya Plastik Indonesia fails to resolve its OTIF and quality issues within the 30-day cure period, the Category Manager will activate Asia Pacific Resin Trading Ltd as an interim dual-source for PET bottle caps, requiring an emergency supplier onboarding (expedited per PRC-SOG-010) and a corresponding RFQ for the affected SKUs.

**Scenario B**: PT Wira Packaging Solutions' sustained "Strategic Partner" performance supports a recommendation to extend their framework agreement with an additional 18-month term and explore a joint cost-reduction initiative on laminate film thickness optimization, to be tabled at the next Procurement Steering Committee meeting.
